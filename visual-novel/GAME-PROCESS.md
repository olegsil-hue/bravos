# How play works — Unmapped Peninsula

Player-facing description of the opening session. Facts from [project-context.md](project-context.md). English in the game; [opening-posts.md](opening-posts.md) stays the Russian archive.

## What this session is

- Unmapped Peninsula is a single opening: **17.07.2025**, ~**20:35**, a tourist bus on a peninsula missing from maps, globes, and the internet.
- The destination is a company mansion vacation. Some passengers paid their own way; some were delivered. The company is unnamed.
- There are no extra main heroes. The cast is **Kane Avis**, **Simon Harrison**, **Mikari / Tsuji**, **Adena / Deni**, and the weasel familiar **Laska**.

## How you enter

- The public hub (`#/`) lists Gameplay, Heroes studio, Suggest room, Game process — plus host/join. Gameplay opens a 30-second briefing (what / why / what awaits), then the bus.
- Host creates a five-character code and a display name. Join needs a code of four or more characters. Play still runs in this browser; no server.
- Keyboard on Gameplay: **Enter** continues, **1–9** pick choices, **Escape** returns to the hub.

## Illustrated introduction

- Illustrated briefing first (what this is, why the trip, what awaits). Play starts on the bus: company recording, immediate seating choice, Kane speaking from the window unasked.
- Seating map: **Mikari beside Adena**; **Simon beside** a similar-age unfriendly man (Kane likely, not proven). Clock ~20:35. Then organizer / attention / arrival choices.

## First scenes with heroes

- Each hero gets an entrance beat matching their post: Kane at the window, Simon with a book, Mikari with headphones trying to sleep, Adena with a headache and Laska in the hoodie on a stuffy bus.
- They speak in English in their own voices. Mikari’s personal notes still read **16.05 / 17.05** while the scene is dated **17.07.2025** — unresolved.
- Laska is allowed because the trip permitted more than luggage. She pokes, yawns, and hides.

## Choices and branches

- After the entrances, chat-style choices change who speaks, where attention sits, and how the short arrival plays.
- **Choice 1 — organizer:** Simon, Adena, Mikari, Kane, or silence.
- **Choice 2 — attention:** the Mikari–Adena pair, Simon’s unnamed unfriendly seatmate, or the window reflection.
- **Choice 3 — arrival:** private rooms (Kane’s plan), linger at the gate, or wait until Laska will walk on gravel.

## Studio and suggest room

- Heroes studio (`#/studio`) is a separate atelier: portrait, name, colors, outfit layer, hair grade, expression, accessory, bio. Saved to localStorage and used in play.
- Suggest room (`#/upgrade`) is a proposal space, not a second HUD. You may assign nerve/fatigue and one suggested perk per hero. Perks show as chips during Gameplay.
- Neither room invents new protagonists.

## What stays open

- What the company is, who paid whose road, and who “should” organize introductions.
- Mikari’s 16.05 / 17.05 versus 17.07.2025.
- The true name of Simon’s seatmate — Kane is likely from age and manner, not a citation.
